## Instructions
1. Determine whether ALL documents belong to the SAME claim. Compare every
   identifier across documents: claim number, policy number, insured /
   claimant / policyholder name, incident / loss date, claim amount, property
   address, and any other field that appears in more than one document. List
   EVERY mismatch as an inconsistency. If any document set mixes more than one
   claim number or policy number, that is ALWAYS at least Medium risk.
2. Identify fraud indicators present in the documents, for example: recent
   policy inception relative to the loss date, vacant or abandoned property,
   delayed reporting or delayed police report, inflated or unusual claim
   amounts, discrepancies between reported and claimed amounts, suspicious or
   contradictory narratives, missing or altered documents, or strong alignment
   with the historical fraud cases above.
3. Decide the overall fraud risk: "Low", "Medium", or "High". Be conservative
   for benign claims but do not downplay real signals.
