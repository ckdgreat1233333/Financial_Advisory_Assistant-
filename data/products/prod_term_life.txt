1. Overview
Pure risk-cover life insurance for a chosen sum assured and policy term. There is no maturity benefit; the nominee receives the sum assured only if the life assured passes away during the term.

2. Key Features
Minimum investment: Rs. 6,000. Liquidity classification: none. Sum assured from Rs. 50 lakh to Rs. 5 crore. Premium rates fixed at entry for the whole term. Riders for critical illness and accident available.

3. Suitability Profile
Recommended for investors with a computed risk capacity of conservative or moderate or aggressive. Investment horizon should be at least 0 months. Aligns with goals: safety. Any earner whose household depends on their income, especially with loans or dependents. Cover of roughly ten times annual income is a common planning benchmark.

4. Risk Factors And Warnings
No money back at any point. Claims can be repudiated for non-disclosure of medical history at proposal stage. Premiums rise steeply if purchased late.

5. Charges Liquidity And Taxation
Annual premium level for the term; GST applies. No surrender value exists because there is no investment component.
