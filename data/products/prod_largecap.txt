1. Overview
An actively managed equity fund investing at least 80 percent of assets in the top 100 listed companies by market capitalisation. Returns depend entirely on stock-market performance.

2. Key Features
Minimum investment: Rs. 1,000. Liquidity classification: medium. Fund manager discretion adds tracking difference versus the index. SIP step-up facility available.

3. Suitability Profile
Recommended for investors with a computed risk capacity of moderate or aggressive. Investment horizon should be at least 36 months. Aligns with goals: wealth, retirement. Moderate-to-aggressive investors comfortable with multi-year equity cycles.

4. Risk Factors And Warnings
Manager risk on top of market risk. Drawdowns similar to the index; poor manager selection can underperform the index over long stretches.

5. Charges Liquidity And Taxation
Expense ratio around 1.0-1.6 percent regular plan. Equity taxation as above.
