1. Overview
An equity-linked savings scheme with a statutory lock-in of 36 months per installment. Investments qualify for Section 80C deduction up to Rs. 1.5 lakh per year under the old tax regime.

2. Key Features
Minimum investment: Rs. 500. Liquidity classification: low. Every installment locks for 36 months from its own date. After lock-in, redemption is fully at the investor's discretion.

3. Suitability Profile
Recommended for investors with a computed risk capacity of moderate or aggressive. Investment horizon should be at least 36 months. Aligns with goals: tax, wealth. Tax-paying investors in the old regime with a minimum three-year commitment.

4. Risk Factors And Warnings
Thirty-six-month lock-in applies even if markets fall, so exit timing is impossible. Full equity volatility applies.

5. Charges Liquidity And Taxation
Expense ratio near 1.0-1.8 percent regular plan. Lock-in prevents exit-load complexity. Equity taxation applies.
