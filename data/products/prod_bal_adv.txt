1. Overview
A dynamically managed hybrid fund that shifts allocation between equity and debt based on market valuation cues. Equity exposure can range from roughly 30 to 80 percent, so NAV moves with markets in both directions.

2. Key Features
Minimum investment: Rs. 1,000. Liquidity classification: medium. SIP supported from Rs. 1,000 per month. Allocation shifts reviewed daily. Exit load 1 percent within 15 days only.

3. Suitability Profile
Recommended for investors with a computed risk capacity of moderate or aggressive. Investment horizon should be at least 36 months. Aligns with goals: wealth, retirement, education. Moderate-risk investors entering equity gradually, or those uneasy with full equity volatility.

4. Risk Factors And Warnings
Equity-leg drawdowns of 10-25 percent are historically normal. Returns are uncertain and never guaranteed.

5. Charges Liquidity And Taxation
Expense ratio around 0.8-1.1 percent regular plan. Equity-oriented taxation: 12.5 percent LTCG beyond exemption.
