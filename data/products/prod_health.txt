1. Overview
Indemnity health insurance covering hospitalisation for the family with cashless treatment across network hospitals, annual renewal, and restoration of sum insured on partial utilisation.

2. Key Features
Minimum investment: Rs. 9,000. Liquidity classification: none. Sum insured options from Rs. 5 lakh to Rs. 1 crore. Pre and post hospitalisation covered 60 and 90 days respectively. Waiting periods apply for pre-existing conditions.

3. Suitability Profile
Recommended for investors with a computed risk capacity of conservative or moderate or aggressive. Investment horizon should be at least 0 months. Aligns with goals: safety. Every household; medical inflation routinely outruns self-insurance capacity.

4. Risk Factors And Warnings
Premiums increase with age band and medical inflation. Co-pay, room-rent caps and disease-specific waiting periods limit certain claims.

5. Charges Liquidity And Taxation
Annual premium revisable by insurer with IRDAI approval; GST applies. No-claim bonus raises sum insured without extra premium.
