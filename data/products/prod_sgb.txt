Goal Tags: wealth

1. Overview
Government securities denominated in grams of gold. They pay a fixed 2.5 percent interest per annum in addition to tracking the market price of gold at redemption. Eight-year maturity with exit windows from year five.

2. Key Features
Minimum investment: Rs. 7,000. Liquidity classification: low. Interest credited half-yearly to the bank account. Tradable on secondary market from year five. Capital gains on redemption are tax-exempt.

3. Suitability Profile
Recommended for investors with a computed risk capacity of moderate or aggressive. Investment horizon should be at least 60 months. Aligns with goals: wealth. Investors wanting gold exposure without storage cost, sized below 15 percent of the portfolio.

4. Risk Factors And Warnings
Gold price can fall sharply and stay down for years. Five-year minimum holding before the first exit window.

5. Charges Liquidity And Taxation
Issued at market gold price plus zero premium; 2.5 percent yearly interest paid by RBI. Redemption proceeds exempt from capital-gains tax.
