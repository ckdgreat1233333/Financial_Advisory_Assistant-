1. Overview
A contributory pension scheme regulated by PFRDA. Contributions are invested across equity, corporate bonds and government securities according to a chosen auto or active life-cycle strategy until age 60.

2. Key Features
Minimum investment: Rs. 500. Liquidity classification: low. Tier-I account is locked until age 60 with partial withdrawal for defined needs after three years. Up to 75 percent equity allowed under active choice.

3. Suitability Profile
Recommended for investors with a computed risk capacity of moderate or aggressive. Investment horizon should be at least 120 months. Aligns with goals: retirement, tax. Salaried earners building a dedicated retirement corpus alongside EPF, wanting extra Section 80CCD(1B) deduction of Rs. 50,000.

4. Risk Factors And Warnings
Locked until 60 except narrow exceptions. Annuity purchase of at least 40 percent of the corpus at exit is mandatory and annuity rates then prevailing apply.

5. Charges Liquidity And Taxation
Charge architecture under 0.1 percent per year plus record-keeping fees. Withdrawal corpus taxed as per prevailing rules; 60 percent lump sum at 60 currently exempt.
