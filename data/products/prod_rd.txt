Subject: Monthly Recurring Deposit
Category: Deposit

Product ID: prod_rd
Risk Level: LOW
Min Investment: Rs. 1,000
Lock-in Period: 12 months
Expected Returns: 6.75-7.1 percent per annum (indicative, NOT guaranteed)
Goal Tags: safety, education, wealth

1. Overview
A deposit where a fixed installment is auto-debited every month for a chosen tenor of 12 to 120 months. At maturity the depositor receives principal plus compounded interest.

2. Key Features
Minimum investment: Rs. 1,000. Liquidity classification: low. Installments auto-debited on a chosen day each month. One missed installment attracts a small penalty; six consecutive misses close the deposit.

3. Suitability Profile
Recommended for investors with a computed risk capacity of conservative or moderate or aggressive. Investment horizon should be at least 12 months. Aligns with goals: safety, education, wealth. Savers building discipline toward a dated goal such as school fees due in one to three years.

4. Risk Factors And Warnings
Missed installments reduce maturity value. Interest taxable at slab. Premature closure converts the deposit to a savings rate for the completed period.

5. Charges Liquidity And Taxation
Penalty of Rs. 50-Rs. 150 per missed installment depending on the amount. TDS rules identical to fixed deposits.
