1. Overview
An open-ended debt scheme investing predominantly in AAA-rated corporate bonds, government securities and money-market instruments. Returns are market-linked and not guaranteed.

2. Key Features
Minimum investment: Rs. 5,000. Liquidity classification: medium. Daily NAV, SIP from Rs. 500 per month, exit load nil beyond seven days. Credit-rating and duration risk apply.

3. Suitability Profile
Recommended for investors with a computed risk capacity of conservative or moderate or aggressive. Investment horizon should be at least 12 months. Aligns with goals: wealth, safety, education. Conservative-to-moderate investors parking funds beyond one year who accept small NAV fluctuation for better post-tax yield than deposits.

4. Risk Factors And Warnings
Not insured or guaranteed. A downgrade of held paper or rising-rate shock can produce negative returns over short windows.

5. Charges Liquidity And Taxation
Expense ratio up to about 0.5 percent direct plan. Exit load nil after seven days. Taxation: LTCG above Rs. 1.25 lakh taxed at 12.5 percent.
