Goal Tags: safety, wealth, education

1. Overview
A term deposit placed with the bank for a fixed tenor at a contracted interest rate. Interest accrues quarterly and may be paid out or compounded. Tenors range from 7 days to 10 years.

2. Key Features
Minimum investment: Rs. 5,000. Liquidity classification: medium. Premature closure permitted with a 0.5-1.0 percent rate penalty. Nomination available. Senior citizens earn 0.50 percent higher interest.

3. Suitability Profile
Recommended for investors with a computed risk capacity of conservative or moderate or aggressive. Investment horizon should be at least 6 months. Aligns with goals: safety, wealth, education. First-time investors, emergency reserves, and capital-preservation allocations near a goal date. Suitable as the stability core of any portfolio.

4. Risk Factors And Warnings
Premature withdrawal earns a lower rate. Interest is fully taxable at slab rate. Bank failure risk mitigated but DICGC insurance caps at Rs. 5 lakh per bank per depositor.

5. Charges Liquidity And Taxation
No entry charge. TDS applies on interest above Rs. 40,000 per year (Rs. 50,000 for seniors) unless Form 15G/15H is filed.
