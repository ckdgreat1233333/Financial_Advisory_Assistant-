Subject: Sweep-in Savings Plus Account
Category: Savings
Version: v1.0
Product ID: prod_sweep
Risk Level: LOW
Min Investment: Rs. 25,000
Lock-in Period: None (open-ended)
Expected Returns: 3.5-4.25 percent per annum (indicative, NOT guaranteed)
Goal Tags: safety

1. Overview
A savings account linked to fixed deposits. Balances above a chosen threshold are automatically swept into FD units in multiples of Rs. 1,000 and reverse-swept when withdrawals exceed the balance.

2. Key Features
Minimum investment: Rs. 25,000. Liquidity classification: high. Fully liquid at par value up to the sweep threshold. FD leg earns deposit rates without breaking the full deposit.

3. Suitability Profile
Recommended for investors with a computed risk capacity of conservative or moderate or aggressive. Investment horizon should be at least 0 months. Aligns with goals: safety. Idle operating balances above one month of expenses that still need same-day access.

4. Risk Factors And Warnings
Swept FD units broken early earn a penalised rate. Returns lag inflation over long periods.

5. Charges Liquidity And Taxation
No account fee beyond the standard schedule of charges. Sweep operations themselves are free.
