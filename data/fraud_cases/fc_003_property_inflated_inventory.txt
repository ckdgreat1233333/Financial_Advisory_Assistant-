CASE_ID: FC-003
CASE_TYPE: Property
FRAUD_LEVEL: Medium
FRAUD_INDICATORS: inflated inventory; duplicate receipts; over-stated replacement value
RESOLUTION: Partial payout after audit; claimant penalized

NARRATIVE:
A homeowner filed a burglary claim listing a large inventory of electronic items, each backed by receipts that appeared uniform in formatting. Several receipts were traced to a single printer and contained identical timestamps. The claimed replacement values were significantly higher than retail market prices for the listed models. The total claimed amount was close to the policy's contents limit. Neighbor statements suggested the property had been empty for several weeks prior to the reported incident.
