Insurance Claims Policy

=========================================
Section 1 - Coverage Scope
=========================================

1. The policy covers claims for Auto, Health, Property, Fire, Theft, Travel and Liability events during the active policy period.

2. Claims must be reported within 30 days of the incident date.

3. A valid claim requires an active policy number and a policy that was in force on the date of the incident.

4. Claims filed within 14 days of policy inception are subject to heightened scrutiny and manual review.

=========================================
Section 2 - Required Documents
=========================================

The following documents are mandatory for claim processing:

• Claim Form (signed by the policyholder)

• Policy Document / Certificate

• Proof of Loss statement

Depending on the claim type, the following documents are also required:

• Medical Report (health claims)

• Police Report (theft / accident claims)

• Invoice / Receipt (property / repair claims)

• Incident Report (fire / property damage claims)

Missing mandatory documents will result in manual review.

=========================================
Section 3 - Coverage Limits
=========================================

1. Claim amounts must not exceed the coverage limit specified in the policy.

2. Claims exceeding the coverage limit are only partially payable.

3. Coverage limits are defined per claim type in the policy schedule.

4. High value claims above the limit require additional verification.

=========================================
Section 4 - Exclusions
=========================================

The following events are excluded from coverage:

1. Intentional damage or self-inflicted loss.

2. Losses caused by pre-existing medical conditions declared on the policy.

3. Claims arising from illegal activity.

4. Normal wear and tear of property or vehicles.

5. Losses occurring outside the policy territory without prior approval.

6. Fraudulent, inflated, or misrepresented claims.

=========================================
Section 5 - Fraud Screening Rules
=========================================

Low Fraud Risk

Claim details consistent across all documents.

Claim amount within coverage limits.

Policy in force for more than 14 days at time of incident.

No historical fraud pattern similarity.

Medium Fraud Risk

Minor inconsistencies between submitted documents.

Claim filed shortly after policy inception.

Claim amount close to the coverage limit.

Similarity to historical fraud cases below alert threshold.

High Fraud Risk

Multiple inconsistencies across submitted documents.

Claim filed within 14 days of policy inception.

Claim amount significantly exceeding coverage limits.

Strong semantic similarity to historical fraud cases.

Missing critical supporting documents.

=========================================
Section 6 - Escalation Rules
=========================================

1. Any claim flagged as High Fraud Risk must be escalated for manual review by a claim officer.

2. Claims flagged as Medium Fraud Risk with coverage uncertainty must also be escalated.

3. A claim officer can override the AI recommendation after reviewing the evidence.

4. Overrides must record the officer's name and reason in the audit trail.

5. No claim is auto-rejected based on fraud screening alone; a human decision is always required.

6. The customer must not be accused of fraud. Communications must state that the claim is under additional verification.

=========================================
Section 7 - Claim Resolution
=========================================

1. Accepted claims are paid up to the coverage limit minus applicable deductibles.

2. Rejected claims must include a clear reason communicated to the customer in plain language.

3. Rejected claims can be appealed by the customer within 30 days.

4. Every decision must include an explanation that the customer can understand.
