Home Loan Policy

=========================================
Section 1 - Eligibility
=========================================

1. Applicant must be between 21 and 60 years of age.

2. Applicant must be an Indian citizen.

3. Applicant must have a valid PAN Card and Aadhaar Card.

4. Applicant must have an active bank account.

5. Applicant must apply using accurate and truthful information.

=========================================
Section 2 - Required Documents
=========================================

The following documents are mandatory:

• Salary Slip (last 3 months)

• Bank Statement (last 6 months)

• Employment Letter

• PAN Card

• Aadhaar Card

Missing mandatory documents will result in manual review.

=========================================
Section 3 - Income Requirements
=========================================

Minimum monthly salary:

₹30,000

Salary should be stable for at least the previous 6 months.

Salary mentioned in the salary slip should reasonably match salary credits visible in the bank statement.

Large unexplained differences must be flagged for manual review.

=========================================
Section 4 - Employment Requirements
=========================================

Applicant should have completed at least 12 months with the current employer.

Applicants changing jobs within the previous 6 months may require additional verification.

Self-employed applicants must provide income tax returns for the previous 2 financial years.

=========================================
Section 5 - Loan Amount Rules
=========================================

Requested loan amount should not exceed 20 times the applicant's monthly salary.

Loan-to-Income ratio should remain within acceptable banking limits.

Higher loan amounts require additional risk assessment.

=========================================
Section 6 - Risk Rules
=========================================

Low Risk

All mandatory documents submitted.

Income verified.

Employment stable.

Loan amount within limits.

Medium Risk

Minor mismatch between salary slip and bank statement.

Recent employment change.

Missing optional information.

High Risk

Missing mandatory documents.

False or inconsistent financial information.

Income below minimum requirement.

Requested loan amount significantly exceeds policy limits.

=========================================
Section 7 - Manual Review Rules
=========================================

Applications shall be sent for manual review if:

Documents are password protected.

OCR confidence is below acceptable threshold.

Income verification cannot be completed.

Multiple policy violations exist.

Financial information is inconsistent.

=========================================
Section 8 - Customer Communication
=========================================

The AI assistant may explain policy decisions using only retrieved policy information.

The assistant must never promise loan approval.

The assistant must recommend contacting a loan officer whenever manual review is required.

If sufficient information is unavailable, the assistant must clearly state that it cannot determine eligibility.
