Subject: Amendment to KYC Master Direction - Periodic Updation
Version: v1.0
Category: circular

1. Amendment to Periodic Updation Frequency
This circular amends clause 1.3 of the Master Direction on Know Your
Customer norms issued under Circular No RBI/2025-26/09. With effect from
the date of this circular, KYC records of all customers, irrespective of
risk classification, shall be updated at least once every five years.

1.1 Application to Existing Accounts
The revised updation frequency shall apply to all accounts existing on the
date of this circular and to all accounts opened thereafter. Branches shall
complete the updation of all existing accounts within the next updation
cycle.

1.2 Consequences of Non-Updation
Where KYC records remain un-updated beyond five years from the last
updation, the bank shall restrict the operation of the account until the
records are updated, and shall communicate the reason to the customer in
writing.

1.3 Supersession
To the extent the provisions of clause 1.3 and clause 1.4 of the earlier
master direction are inconsistent with this circular, the provisions of
this circular shall prevail.
