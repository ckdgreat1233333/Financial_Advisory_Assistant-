Circular No: RBI/2025-26/14
Date: 15-Sep-2025
Issued By: Department of Regulation
Subject: Disclosure of Charges and Fees for Banking Services
Version: v1.0
Category: circular

1. Disclosure of Charges
Banks shall display a complete schedule of service charges and fees on
their website and at every branch in a customer-friendly format. The
schedule shall list each charge, the basis on which the charge is levied,
and the frequency of levying the charge.

1.1 Prior Notice of Revision
No bank shall revise or introduce any charge or fee without giving the
customer prior notice of at least thirty days before the effective date.
The notice shall be sent through SMS, email or letter and shall clearly
state the existing charge and the revised charge.

1.2 Customer Consent
Banks shall obtain explicit consent from the customer before levying any
new charge on an existing product or service. In the absence of such
consent, the bank shall provide the customer the option to close the
account or switch to an alternate product without penalty.

1.3 Penal Charges on Loans
Penal interest or penal charges on loans shall be based on the outstanding
amount for which default occurred and shall not be a means of revenue
enhancement. Excessive penal charges that are not proportionate to the
delayed amount shall be treated as usury.

1.4 Communication of Changes
Whenever a bank revises its schedule of charges, it shall update the
website and mobile application within seven days of the effective date and
shall communicate a summary of changes to customers along with the monthly
statement or by other electronic means.
