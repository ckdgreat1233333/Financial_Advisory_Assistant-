Circular No: RBI/2024-25/77
Date: 12-Mar-2025
Issued By: Department of Regulation
Subject: Customer Grievance Redressal Framework
Version: v1.0
Category: circular

1. Internal Grievance Redressal
Every bank shall have a dedicated internal grievance redressal mechanism
and shall designate a nodal officer to acknowledge and resolve customer
complaints. Complaints may be lodged through the branch, the call centre,
the website or the mobile application.

1.1 Acknowledgement and Resolution Timelines
Banks shall acknowledge every complaint within three working days of
receipt. The bank shall resolve the complaint and communicate the decision
to the customer within thirty days from the date of receipt of the
complaint. If additional information is required, the timeline shall be
recomputed from the date of receipt of such information.

1.2 Escalation to Internal Ombudsman
If the customer is not satisfied with the bank's response, the customer may
approach the Internal Ombudsman of the bank within thirty days of receiving
the bank's decision. The Internal Ombudsman shall review the complaint and
issue a recommendation within thirty days of receipt.

1.3 Regulatory Reporting
Banks shall report the number and value of complaints received, resolved
and pending to the Reserve Bank of India on a quarterly basis. Unresolved
complaints beyond ninety days shall be highlighted separately in the
report.

1.4 Service Quality Standards
Banks shall ensure that the grievance redressal process does not require
the customer to repeatedly provide the same information. Complaints shall
not be rejected solely on the ground that the customer did not follow a
specific format, provided the complaint contains the essential details.
