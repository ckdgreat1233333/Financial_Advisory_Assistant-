Subject: Internal Policy - KYC Implementation Guidelines for Branch Staff
Issued By: Compliance Department
Version: v2.1
Category: internal_policy

1. Customer Onboarding Procedure
Branch staff shall complete customer identity verification at the time of
account opening using the bank's approved document verification application.
A photocopy or electronic image of the verifying document shall be captured
and stored in the customer record. Onboarding shall be rejected when the
verification document cannot be authenticated.

2. Verification Steps for Walk-in Customers
For walk-in customers, staff shall check the identity document against the
central KYC database, confirm the photograph matches the applicant, and
record the customer's risk classification in the onboarding system before
activating the account.

3. Document Requirements for High Risk Accounts
Accounts classified as high risk shall require two identity documents and
a proof of address, together with a declaration of the source of funds.
The branch manager shall approve such accounts before activation.

4. Handling Requests for KYC Updation
When a customer approaches for KYC updation, staff shall verify the
documents afresh, update the risk classification if warranted, and record
the updation in the system with the date and time. Customers shall be
informed of the outcome within five working days.

5. Escalation for Unverified Records
Where KYC records are pending updation for more than the regulatory
timeline, staff shall flag the account in the system and seek approval
from the compliance team before restricting account operations. The
customer shall be informed before any restriction is applied.
