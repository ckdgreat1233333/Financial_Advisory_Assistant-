Subject: KYC Audit Checklist for Internal Audit Teams
Issued By: Internal Audit Department
Version: v1.3
Category: audit_checklist

1. Customer Acceptance Policy
Verify that the board-approved customer acceptance policy is documented,
available to all branches, and consistent with the latest RBI master
direction on KYC.

2. CDD Verification Sample
Select a sample of at least thirty accounts opened in the quarter and
verify that customer identity was verified using an officially valid
document and that the verification was recorded in the system.

3. Risk Classification Coverage
Verify that every account carries a risk classification (low, medium or
high) and that enhanced due diligence was applied for high risk accounts
and politically exposed persons.

4. KYC Updation Compliance
Verify that KYC updation was performed within the prescribed frequency for
each risk category and that accounts with pending updation were flagged.

5. Record Retention Checks
Verify that customer identification records and transaction records are
retained for the prescribed period and that the retention register is
maintained and signed by the branch manager.

6. Staff Training Records
Verify that all staff handling customer onboarding have completed the
mandatory KYC and anti-money laundering training in the current year and
that training records are available for inspection.

7. Escalation and Exception Log
Verify that exceptions, deferrals and escalations in the KYC process are
logged with reasons and that the compliance team reviewed the exception
log in the current quarter.
