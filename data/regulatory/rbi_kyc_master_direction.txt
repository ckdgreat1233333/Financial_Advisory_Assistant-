Circular No: RBI/2025-26/09
Date: 01-Aug-2025
Issued By: Department of Regulation
Subject: Master Direction - Know Your Customer (KYC) Norms
Version: v1.0
Category: circular

1. Applicability
These directions shall apply to all Scheduled Commercial Banks, including
regional rural banks and urban cooperative banks, in respect of their
customer acquisition and account operations. Every bank shall implement a
robust KYC framework covering customer acceptance, customer due diligence,
ongoing monitoring and record keeping.

1.1 Customer Due Diligence
Before opening an account or establishing a business relationship, a bank
shall identify the customer and verify the customer's identity using
reliable and independent sources. For individual customers, verification
shall be based on any one officially valid document such as a passport,
driving licence, Voter ID, Aadhaar card or NREGA job card.

1.2 Risk-Based Approach
Banks shall classify customers into low, medium and high risk categories
based on risk perception. Simplified due diligence shall apply to low risk
customers while enhanced due diligence, including verification of the
source of funds and beneficial ownership, shall apply to high risk
customers and to politically exposed persons.

1.3 Periodic Updation of KYC Records
KYC records of low risk customers shall be updated at least once every ten
years and records of medium and high risk customers shall be updated at
least once every eight years. On failure to update KYC records within the
stipulated period, the bank may restrict the operation of the account.

1.4 Record Retention
Banks shall preserve all records pertaining to the identification of
customers and their account profiles for a period of five years after the
business relationship has ended. Records of transactions shall be preserved
for a minimum of five years from the date of the transaction.

1.5 E-KYC
Banks may carry out electronic KYC using the Aadhaar authentication service
or the DigiLocker facility subject to the consent of the customer and
compliance with the Aadhaar (Targeted Delivery of Financial and Other
Subsidies, Benefits and Services) Act, 2016.
